The problem

Paper tickets and plastic cards cause problems every day

Lost tickets and cards

Drivers lose them, argue at the exit, and pay a fine. The queue behind them waits.

Cash that's hard to check

Matching the cash in each exit booth to the cars that left is slow, and gaps are hard to explain.

Paper and cards to replace

Ticket rolls to buy, lost cards to replace, and ticket machines that jam on busy nights.

How it works

In with a scan. Out with a tap.

  1. 1

    Scan at the gate

    The driver scans the QR sign at the entrance with their phone camera. The parking session starts and the barrier opens.

  2. 2

    Ticket on the phone

    It's saved in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet and shows the time parked and the fee so far. Shop, eat, take your time. It can't be lost.

  3. 3

    Scan at the exit machine

    At the exit, the driver holds the ticket QR up to the kiosk. It calculates the fee and shows it on screen.

  4. 4

    Tap your card, drive out

    The driver taps, inserts or swipes a card on the machine beside the kiosk. The barrier opens and the ticket closes, so it can't be used again.

For parking and operations managers

Every car and every pound, accounted for

  • Exits that run without staff. A kiosk and a card machine on each exit lane. Keep one staffed lane for cash and paper tickets.
  • Every pound accounted for. Card payments go straight to your account. On the staffed lane, you know exactly how much cash each person should hand over.
  • A ticket can only be paid once. Free exits need a written reason, and every action is logged.
  • No new cameras, no ticket machines. A QR sign at the entrance, and a kiosk with a card machine at the exit, connected to your existing barriers.
  • Your own prices. Free minutes, hourly rates, daily caps or a flat fee, all calculated automatically.

Questions managers ask

What about drivers without a smartphone?

Your paper ticket button stays. PARQ is an extra option, not a replacement on day one.

Do drivers need to download an app?

No. The ticket opens in the phone's browser and can be saved to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet in one tap.

Do we have to change our barriers?

No. We connect the entrance QR and the exit kiosk to your existing barriers, together with your technician.

What about drivers who want to pay cash?

Keep one staffed exit lane for cash and paper tickets. The kiosk lanes take card, Meeza and phone payments.

What if a driver's phone dies?

Every ticket has a short code. The driver can type it on the kiosk, or press the help button, and the normal lost-ticket process still applies.

Do you collect drivers' personal data?

No names, phone numbers or plates are needed. A ticket is just a random code, a time and a fee.

What does it cost after the pilot?

A simple monthly fee per lane, agreed with you after the pilot, based on the results.
